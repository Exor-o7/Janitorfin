TV Show Rules
Use -1 to disable a specific TV cleanup rule. Episode eligibility is still evaluated per episode, then applied to the selected season or series scope.
Season is the recommended default because TV content is usually managed per season. Janitorfin only stages episodes when every episode in the chosen season or series scope is eligible.

Operations
Preview shows media that matches the current form settings. It does not queue or delete anything.
Cleanup saves the current form values, then starts one of Janitorfin's scheduled tasks. Scan Pending updates the pending list; Delete Due Pending removes only items whose saved grace deadline has elapsed.
Pending List shows media already staged for deletion. Janitorfin also mirrors these items into the Jellyfin collection Removing Soon.
